How to reach the right place

Rights holders & removal requests

If you are a rights holder or upstream project maintainer and believe material hosted by this project should be removed or reviewed, please contact us. Include:

  • the exact material — build name or URL — you are asking about
  • your relationship to the rights involved
  • what action you are requesting: review or removal
  • a way for us to reply to you

We will review the request and may remove the affected material. We do not claim ownership of the upstream projects included in these builds, and asking never hurts.

Where to reach us

The fastest reliable channel is a GitHub issue on the project repository, which keeps a public, timestamped record.

[[component:github-icon]]Open a GitHub issue

Sensitive requests — anything you would rather not post publicly — can say so in the issue; we will move to a private channel for the follow-up. No private information is exposed on this site, and we ask that reports do not include unrelated personal data. Response time depends on the maintainer's availability: this is a one-person project with no guaranteed schedule.
