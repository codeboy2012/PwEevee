  • Apple Music's red joins Spotify's green under the accent colour, under either look

  • listening stats kept on the phone, with Spotify's own export imported
  • live activity

  • local files get lyrics in the redesigned player
  • lyrics can play as the lock screen's animated artwork, a clip per line
  • lyrics translate into any language, from Musixmatch's community or, with your own Gemini key, any song on a tap
  • mini player in the tab bar, Apple Music style
  • Mod > App icon changes the home screen icon to a Liquid Glass one or the logo in another colour, on iOS 26
  • Mod Settings in the system Settings' style, with its own Account page
  • more visualisations
  • Music Haptics keep going in the background and on the lock screen, through iOS's own Music Haptics

What it is

Spotify, in glass.

A no-jailbreak Theos tweak that rebuilds Spotify for iOS around a Liquid Glass design: a glass tab bar, player and lyrics, a Mod Settings panel, Live Activity, accent colours, gestures, blocked artists, and speed and pitch control.

Liquid Glass redesign of the Spotify iOS interface

Mod Settings panel and Live Activity support

Accent colours, gestures, blocked artists, speed & pitch

Builds from source with Theos exactly as upstream documents

Inside a build

How this component is used

What ships

A compatible build of the tweak is installed into the app as Frameworks/spotifyglass.dylib, with its MobileSubstrate filter and the CydiaSubstrate.framework that the proven upstream build ships alongside it.

The exact version integrated in each build is recorded in that build's manifest and shown on the download.

Building it from source

Building the upstream tweak from source works exactly as upstream documents it, with Theos on macOS. PwEevee's own pipeline does not replace or fork that process — it consumes a compatible build of the result.

See docs/tweaks.md in the repository and the upstream repository itself.
