Ecosystem

Related projects. Separate projects.

A PwEevee build is the product of three codebases with three different owners. This page keeps them distinct: what each one is, who wrote it, where its source lives, and what its latest published release is.

The three projects

PwEevee

Maintained here

One tested Spotify IPA carrying two upstream tweaks.

PwEevee is the integration layer: it obtains and prepares compatible upstream components, resolves their dependencies (duplicates fail the build), injects the tweaks into a decrypted Spotify binary, validates Mach-O load commands and filesystem structure, packages a reproducible IPA with a full build manifest, and publishes the result through GitHub releases with its exact SHA-256.

spoti.pw

Upstream project

Spotify, in glass.

A no-jailbreak Theos tweak that rebuilds Spotify for iOS around a Liquid Glass design: glass tab bar, player and lyrics, Mod Settings, Live Activity, accent colours, gestures, blocked artists, and speed & pitch controls.

EeveeSpotify

Upstream project

Premium UI unlock, icons and integration fixes.

An upstream Spotify iOS tweak providing premium UI unlock, custom app icons, Siri and media-integration fixes, an offline helper, and theme and localization resources. It is continued as EeveeSpotifyReincarnated by SideloadLabs.

Relationship

How the three fit together

PwEevee — maintained here

The integration layer and the only part this project authored: build pipeline, dependency resolver, Mach-O injection and validation, packaging, release infrastructure, hosting and this website.

spoti.pw — upstream component

An independent project by its own author. A compatible build ships inside the IPA as Frameworks/spotifyglass.dylib. PwEevee integrates it; it does not maintain it.

EeveeSpotify — upstream component

An independent project by its own author. The deb upstream distributes is integrated by PwEevee as Frameworks/EeveeSpotify.dylib with its frameworks, bundle and icons.

PwEevee does not own, control or speak for the upstream projects. Bug reports and feature requests for spoti.pw or EeveeSpotify belong in their own repositories.
