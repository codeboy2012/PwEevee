This page makes no claim of legal immunity, does not assert that attribution grants distribution rights, and does not promise any specific legal outcome.

4. Release archive

This website hosts no build files of its own. Every download it offers resolves to a published release asset on GitHub releases, or to the download URL named by the upstream source that publishes it — for EeveeSpotify IPA variants, the SideloadLabs AltSource. Where a release has no downloadable asset, the site says so instead of offering a broken link.

No permanent availability of any build is promised. We do not delete GitHub releases ourselves, we cannot promise GitHub will retain releases forever, and we have no control over files published by third parties such as SideloadLabs.

We cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability of the project or of historical releases.

Previous project accounts may have become unavailable because of platform Terms of Service issues. Platform decisions and account availability are outside our control. Nothing here accuses any platform of wrongdoing or promises immunity from platform enforcement.
